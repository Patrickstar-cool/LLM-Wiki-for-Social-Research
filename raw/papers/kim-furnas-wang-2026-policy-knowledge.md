# The Production of Policy Knowledge in the United States（美国政策知识的生产）

**Authors:** Taegyoon Kim, Alexander Furnas, Dashun Wang

**Venue:** arXiv:2608.01514 [physics.soc-ph], 2026-08-02

## Abstract

Policymaking relies on institutions that translate expertise into politically actionable knowledge. Yet little is known about how such knowledge is created, structured, and politically polarized, or how science shapes these processes. Using 2 million U.S. policy documents and nearly 1 million scientific citations from more than 200 think tanks and 100 government organizations, we analyze the supply side of science in policymaking: the production of policy knowledge. We find that think tanks are the dominant suppliers of science-based policy knowledge to government, but that this production has become increasingly politically polarized, driven primarily by growing insularity among left-leaning institutions. We further find that science is linked to lower polarization, as policy documents grounded in scientific evidence, especially those citing high-impact science, are less ideologically segregated and occupy more central positions in policy knowledge networks. These results reveal the potential of scientific expertise to shape and, at times, bridge the ideological landscape of policy knowledge production. Amid rising political polarization and the growing role of science in policymaking, these findings are crucial for understanding how policy knowledge production can either reinforce or bridge political divides.

## Key Concepts

### 研究对象：政策知识的「供给侧」
- 政策制定依赖将专业知识转化为「政治上可行动的知识」的制度
- 本研究分析的是**知识生产**（供给端），不是知识使用（需求端）
- 数据：200 万份美国政策文档 + 近 100 万条科学引用；来源：200+ 智库、100+ 政府机构

### 三大发现
1. **智库主导**：智库是向政府供给「基于科学的政策知识」的主导者
2. **极化上升**：政策知识生产日益政治极化——主要由**左倾机构日益「内倾」（insularity）**驱动（同类引用、封闭网络）
3. **科学是去极化力量**：以科学证据为基础的政策文档——尤其引用高影响力科学——意识形态隔离更低、在政策知识网络中占据更中心位置

### 意义
- 科学专业知识有潜力**塑造甚至桥接**政策知识生产的意识形态格局
- 在政治极化和科学角色上升并行的时代：政策知识生产可能强化分裂，也可能架起桥梁
