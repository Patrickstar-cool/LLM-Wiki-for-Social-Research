# Digital State Capacity（数字国家能力）

**Authors:** Patrick Healy, Simon D. Angus, Paul Raschky, Klaus Ackermann, Nathan Lane, Weijia Li, Cynthia Huang

**Venue:** arXiv:2608.03221 [econ.GN], 2026-08-04（171 页，42 图，76 表，含附录）

**Related DOI:** https://doi.org/10.2139/ssrn.7204582（SSRN 工作论文版）

## Abstract

Digital State Capacity is the ability of governments to deploy ICT infrastructure and information systems to implement policy. This paper introduces a new measure of government ICT capacity based on an observable stock of deployable public-sector network infrastructure: public IPv4 address space held by government organisations. These address holdings are key inputs into digital administration because they support internet-facing systems, networked information exchange, and coordination across agencies and functions. The core panel covers approximately 150,000 country-entity records classified as government across more than 150 countries from 2019 to 2024 and can be disaggregated by administrative level and government function. In the 2019 to 2024 Admin-1 panel, government IP holdings are observed in 1,681 subnational regions across all years. We validate the measure at the crosscountry and subnational levels and apply it to government tasks related to corruption control and vaccination rollout. In illustrative country-year analysis, higher Digital State Capacity is associated with higher-quality governance and public-service outcomes in the expected directions, including lower measured corruption and higher vaccination coverage.

## Key Concepts

### 测量创新
- **数字国家能力的定义**：政府部署 ICT 基础设施与信息系统以执行政策的能力
- **新代理指标**：政府组织持有的**公共 IPv4 地址空间**——可观察的「可部署公共部门网络基础设施存量」
- IP 地址是数字行政的关键投入：支撑面向互联网的系统、网络化信息交换、跨机构跨职能协调

### 数据规模
- 核心面板：约 150,000 条「国家-实体」记录，分类为政府，覆盖 150+ 国家，2019–2024
- 可按行政层级（Admin-1 省级）和政府职能细分
- Admin-1 面板：1,681 个次国家级区域，所有年份都有观测

### 验证与应用
- 在国家层面和次国家层面双重验证指标
- 应用场景：腐败控制、疫苗接种覆盖
- 示范性发现：数字国家能力越高 → 治理质量越高、公共服务结果越好（低腐败测量值、高疫苗接种覆盖率）
