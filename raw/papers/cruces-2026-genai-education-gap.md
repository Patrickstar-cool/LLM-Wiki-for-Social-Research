# Does generative AI narrow education-based productivity gaps? Evidence from a randomized experiment（生成式 AI 是否缩小基于教育的生产力差距？随机实验证据）

**Authors:** Guillermo Cruces (Universidad de San Andrés & University of Nottingham), Diego Fernández Meijide (Universidad de San Andrés), Sebastian Galiani (University of Maryland & NBER), Ramiro H. Gálvez, María Lombardi (Universidad Torcuato Di Tella)

**Venue:** arXiv:2608.04198 [econ.GN], submitted 2026-08-04

**AEA-RCT Registry:** 0016607（预注册）

## Abstract

Does generative artificial intelligence (AI) reinforce or reduce productivity differences across workers? Existing evidence largely studies AI within firms and occupations, where organizational selection compresses educational heterogeneity, leaving unclear whether AI narrows productivity gaps across individuals with different levels of education. A related question is whether the productivity gains from AI reflect productive use of the tool or mere delegation, fading once AI is unavailable, and whether the answer differs across education groups.

We address both questions using a randomized online experiment outside firms, in which 1,174 adults aged 25–45 complete an incentivized, workplace-style business problem-solving task with or without a generative-AI assistant, followed by a non-AI-assisted follow-up module. AI increases performance for all participants, with substantially larger gains for lower-education individuals.

In the control group without AI, higher-education participants outperform lower-education participants by 0.548 standard deviations; with AI, this gap falls to 0.139 standard deviations, closing about three-quarters of the initial gap.

Chat-log measures explain both why this gap narrows and why it does not disappear: lower-education participants successfully obtain substantial assistance from AI, while higher-education participants use the tool somewhat more effectively across several margins. The follow-up results show that these gains are not purely driven by delegation; treated participants do not perform worse than controls once AI is removed, and lower-education participants retain part of their gain, although a sizable education gap re-emerges. Consistent with this interpretation, intensive use of the assistant produces strong task performance regardless of how much time and effort participants devote themselves, but follow-up performance is substantially higher only when intensive AI use is combined with sustained effort on the task.

We interpret these findings as evidence that generative AI narrows effective productivity differences in task execution while underlying human-capital differences continue to shape unassisted performance and effective use of the tool.

**JEL Codes:** J24, O33 | **Keywords:** Productivity, artificial intelligence, education, human capital, inequality

## Key Concepts / 关键要点（中文）

### 研究设计
- **为什么不在企业内部做**：现有证据多来自企业与职业内部，组织筛选（hiring/task assignment）会压缩教育异质性——无法回答「AI 是缩小还是扩大教育带来的生产力差距」这一不平等争论的核心问题。本研究刻意用「组织现实主义的权衡」换取教育异质性的识别。
- 1,174 名阿根廷成年人（25–45 岁），预注册随机在线实验；任务为工作场景的商业问题解决（阅读老板邮件、分析多源信息、撰写诊断+解决方案回复），自足、不依赖领域知识，覆盖阅读/数据理解/推理/创意/写作多认知维度。
- AI 助手基于 GPT-4.1，嵌入实验界面；提交后所有人完成**无 AI 的后续模块**（根因开放题 + 内容回忆题）——作为「委托 vs 生产性使用」的诊断。
- 教育分组（预注册）：低教育 = 仅高中文凭或大学（含非大学高等教育）完成不足一半；高教育 = 完成过半。阿根廷开放入学/免费公立高等教育使中间群体异质性很大。

### 核心发现（数字）
1. **AI 提升所有人表现，低教育组获益更大**：低教育组 +1.242 SD，高教育组 +0.834 SD（相对低教育对照组的 SD）
2. **差距关闭约四分之三**：无 AI 时高教育比低教育高 0.548 SD；有 AI 时降至 0.139 SD
3. **差距未消失**：高教育组在 prompt 细节、工作流结构等多个边际上更有效地使用工具（chat-log 证据）
4. **非纯委托**：移除 AI 后治疗组不低于对照组；低教育组保留部分增益（约 +0.171 SD），但 0.200 SD 的教育差距重新出现
5. **AI 使用 × 投入的交互**：高强度 AI 使用 → 当期表现强（无论投入多少）；但后续表现只有「高强度 AI + 高投入」才显著更高——AI 是辅助而非替代努力

### 理论对话
- **技能民主化 vs 技能偏向**：支持 Autor (2024) 的 "skill-democratizing" 潜力（AI 使有限正式训练的工人能做以前需要高教育的任务）；呼应 Acemoglu (2024) 的谨慎——差距未消失、底层人力资本仍塑造无辅助表现。
- **机制 = 任务简化（task simplification）/ 专业水平拉平（expertise-leveling）**：AI 替代低教育者稀缺的输入（抽象推理、复杂书面沟通经验），与 Autor & Thompson (2025)、Althoff & Reichardt (2026) 一致。
- **委托风险文献**：Bastani et al. (2025)、Shen & Tamkin (2026)、Liu et al. (2026) 显示 AI 辅助可降低后续无辅助表现；本研究的结果不支持纯委托解释。
- **历史背景**：技术变革长期是技能偏向的（Acemoglu & Autor 2011; Goldin & Katz 2008），GenAI 是「更直接执行复杂认知任务」的新范式。
