# Transnational political violence in African borderlands（非洲边境地带的跨国政治暴力）

**Authors:** David G. Russell, Olivier J. Walther

**Venue:** arXiv:2608.03596 [econ.GN], 2026-08-04

**Related DOI:** https://doi.org/10.4324/9781003519775（关联书籍章节）

## Abstract

This paper examines the relationship between borderlands and political violence in Africa. Using spatiotemporal data on conflict events from 1997 to 2024 alongside the innovative OECD Spatial Conflict Dynamics indicator (SCDi), it suggests that borderlands see more conflict than non-borderlands. Political violence tends to decrease very rapidly with the distance to borders in Africa. The share of violent events recorded in borderlands varies over time and mirrors the cycles of violence that affect specific regions. It was very high in the early 2000s due to the wars in the Gulf of Guinea and Great Lakes region, declined for less than a decade, and increased substantially in the 2010s. Finally, the paper shows that when conflicts diffuse across borders, they tend to occupy a greater spatial extent, rather than relocate from one side of the border to the other.

## Key Concepts

### 研究问题
- 边境地带（borderlands）与政治暴力是什么关系？——非洲视角
- 数据：1997–2024 冲突事件时空数据 + OECD **空间冲突动态指标（SCDi）**

### 核心发现
1. **边境地带冲突更多**：边境地带比非边境地带经历更多政治暴力
2. **快速距离衰减**：非洲政治暴力随距边境距离增加而**极速下降**
3. **时间波动**：边境地带暴力事件份额随时间变化，镜像特定区域的暴力周期——2000 年代初极高（几内亚湾与大湖地区战争），随后下降不到十年，2010 年代大幅上升
4. **扩散机制**：冲突跨国扩散时，倾向于**占据更大的空间范围**，而不是从边境一侧「迁移」到另一侧

### 意义
- 边境不是冲突的「边缘」——它是冲突的地理磁铁
- 国家中心主义冲突研究需要空间化：边界距离是暴力分布的一阶结构因素
